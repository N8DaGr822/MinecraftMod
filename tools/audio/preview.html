Darkspawn · Audio workshop

Dark atmospheric scores and creature voices. Choose a boss to compare its three intensity levels, or audition a creature’s cues. Nothing plays automatically.

Encounter music

In game: Music volume controls the score. Enter within 64 blocks; leave beyond 80 to fade out. Boss phase changes crossfade. Death, dimension changes, and disconnects end the encounter score.

Creature voices

Original synthesized recordings, shared by region with size-based pitch in game. This player applies that pitch to the sample; Minecraft adds distance and category volume. Combat warnings have subtitles.

Preview recordings are ready for listening. Final mix balance, client crossfades, and spatial cues need an in-game listening pass.
