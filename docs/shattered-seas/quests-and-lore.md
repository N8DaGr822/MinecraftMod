# Optional quests and lore

Status: future design. Named characters, plot details and endings remain proposals from the [source brief](design-brief.md).

## Activities and consequences

Discover optional work through dialogue, taverns, boards, maps, journals, wrecks, exploration and reputation. Support bounties, treasure hunts, faction contracts, cargo delivery/recovery/escort/theft, wreck exploration, legendary captain hunts, haunted expeditions and crew quests.

- **A Pirate's Debt:** recover Corsair gold stolen by the Iron Fleet; return it, sell it or keep it, with appropriate consequences.
- **The King's Law:** hunt a Piglin first mate, then decide whether to expose evidence of the issuing officer's cargo trafficking.
- **Dead Men Tell Tales:** a ghost ship journal leads to an uncharted island, ancient vault and a piece of the mystery.
- **A Ship of Your Own:** optional salvager, merchant or pirate routes to equivalent starter access; see [progression](player-progression.md).

Recommend stable quest IDs, server-validated objectives and once-only completion/rewards. Define per-player versus shared objectives, abandonment and mutually exclusive outcomes before implementing a contract engine. Reuse the Journal for discovered information where suitable; it is not already a quest system.

## The Seven Seals

Seven ancient captains entered the Blackwater Expanse, found a power beneath the sea and returned changed. Their alliance collapsed into fleet warfare and a supernatural disaster: **The Night of the Broken Tide**. Seven scattered seals are believed to reveal treasure or power, but their separation contains an ancient entity. Reassembling or misusing them could weaken its prison. The entity and mechanics are undecided.

Do not use **Heart of the Deep** as the central mystery name. The Siren's Crown, Black Compass, Leviathan's Eye and Dreadwake are possible separate artifacts or vessels, not finalized substitutes. The Logbound Compass is the navigation proposal, not automatically this story's Black Compass.

Gildtusk seeks treasure through maps and buried journals. Blackthorn seeks weapons and control through archives and expeditions. Hollow-Eye remembers the disaster and offers lost memories and clues to the truth. A surviving original captain manipulating the factions is an optional twist, not settled canon.

Reveal clues through different routes and in flexible order: a Corsair map, stolen Fleet chart, Drowned journal or direct discovery may lead to the same island. No requirement to finish every faction's quests or the central mystery.

## Legendary characters and outcomes

Gildtusk, Blackthorn and Hollow-Eye each need a personality, distinctive gear, crew, headquarters or vessel, related quests, rewards and lore. Not every named captain must be fought.

Proposed endings: **Pirate King** claims the power; **Liberator** releases and defeats the entity; **Eternal Guardian** restores its prison and gives up the seals' power. Preserve continued sandbox play in every ending. Multiplayer ownership of story outcomes, replay rules and world reactions need a decision before implementation.

The first narrative slice should contain one environmental clue, one journal and a gateway symbol connection, not a full seven-part campaign. The first 30-minute onboarding sequence in the brief is a pacing target, not timed gating: arrive/explore, discover work, try an activity, then freely choose a voyage or encounter.
