Shared OneBlock dimension: future design notes
Recorded: 2026-10-12

STATUS
The user selected OneBlock implementation on 2026-10-12 and deferred proficiency.
Current decisions and behavior are described in README. The discussion history
below preserves the earlier open proposals; it is not the current contract.

IMPLEMENTATION DECISIONS - USER CONFIRMED
- Free inventory-menu entry and return; no craftable anchor requirement.
- One shared island with a 3x3 dirt footing and a protected regenerating source.
- Separate items only; keep character upgrades. Inventory, worn/stored armor,
  extra storage rows, and Ender Chest contents have independent profiles.
- Death respawns on the island and follows the server's keepInventory rule,
  including ordinary void item loss. No graves or automatic item recovery.
- Six phases: Grassland (100), Stone (200), Farmland (250), Deep Caves (350),
  Nether (450), and endless mixed End. Counts are per phase.
- Thresholds unlock manual advancement when the family is ready. Earlier
  essentials remain available. Include occasional supply chests and vanilla mobs.
- Keep this first implementation classic OneBlock: no custom bosses, extra
  islands, special events, or rewards exported to the outside world.

USER-SELECTED DIRECTION
- Play OneBlock within the existing server, without creating another server.
- Use one shared island. The user wants a cohesive family adventure rather than
  personal islands that make the experience feel competitive.
- Keep the initial experience classic OneBlock. Do not add Darkspawn bosses or
  custom event systems to the initial scope.
- Prevent players from arriving with a full outside inventory and bypassing the
  challenge. Existing-world gear must not trivialize OneBlock progression.
- Special events and distant islands are possible later additions only. No event,
  additional island, or related implementation is selected yet.

WORKING CONCEPT FROM THE DISCUSSION
These are the proposed mechanics supporting the selected direction, rather than
finalized inventory, progression, or death contracts.
- A dedicated dimension with one shared starting island and regenerating block.
- Breaking the central block draws from phase-specific resource selections,
  including appropriate blocks, supply chests, and creature encounters.
- Resource phases and island unlocks belong to the group. Returning players join
  the island's current progress without personal phase-unlock requirements.
- Individual OneBlock inventories persist between visits; shared chests allow
  players to equip and support one another.
- Entering stores the player's outside inventory/equipment and restores their
  OneBlock profile. Leaving stores OneBlock possessions and restores the outside
  profile. Extra inventory rows and planned armor loadouts must be accounted for.
- The island and its progress persist when everyone leaves.
- Provide a dependable return route that does not rely on retaining a single item.

PROPOSALS STILL OPEN

Phase progression
- Automatic advancement versus manual activation is UNDECIDED. The assistant
  recommended unlocking the next phase after sufficient block breaks, then letting
  someone activate it when the family is ready; the user has not chosen this rule.
- Under that proposal, gathering continues in the current phase while waiting.
- Illustrative phases discussed: basic dirt/wood/food; stone/coal/metals; farming
  and animals; dangerous depths; Nether and beyond. No exact roster, order, block
  count, objective, or final encounter is approved.
- Milestone objectives alongside block counts were suggested, but must not expand
  the selected classic OneBlock scope without a further decision.
- Keep essential early resources obtainable after advancing; decide how.

Inventory and progression isolation
- No ordinary item transfers in either direction was recommended, but exact import
  and export rules remain undecided. Outside gear bypass is explicitly unwanted.
- A separate OneBlock progression profile was recommended so outside boss hearts,
  proficiency bonuses, and equipment unlocks do not grant a large head start.
- Decide which health, XP, hunger, skill, inventory-capacity, and unlock states are
  separate. Isolation beyond inventory has not been explicitly approved.
- Decide how potion/meal effects, mounts, accessories, stored armor sets, carried
  containers, and other transfer paths are handled.
- Cosmetic completion rewards usable outside were suggested, not selected.

Travel and death
- Portal/entrance acquisition, placement, and access requirements are undecided.
- A menu return action with a short delay and combat restrictions was suggested;
  the actual travel interface and restrictions remain undecided.
- Death, void falls, respawn location, keepInventory behavior, and recovery rules
  are undecided. A recoverable grave near the central anchor was suggested, not
  approved. Do not silently implement custom graves or item-loss protection.
- Disconnect/reconnect should preserve the correct location and inventory profile.
- Entering, leaving, death, and restart recovery must never duplicate items or
  drop/restore the wrong profile.

IMPLEMENTATION CONSIDERATIONS FOR FUTURE PLANNING
- Inspect current dimension/portal work, including the user's pirate destination,
  and existing inventory/persistence patterns before choosing an implementation.
- Persist shared island state independently of individual player profiles.
- Make profile switching robust across interrupted transitions, saves, deaths,
  disconnects, and server restarts; define recovery before implementation.
- Ensure concurrent block breaks and simultaneous arrivals cannot duplicate
  rewards or corrupt phase progression.
- Protect the central regenerating block from permanent loss or relocation.
- Define safe spawn positions and creature placement when island space is limited.
- Resolve unintended transfer routes through containers, entities, teleportation,
  and other dimensions without changing unrelated gameplay.
- Test multiple players, absent/returning players, repeated travel, full inventories,
  deaths, void falls, reloads, and preservation of the outside character's items.
- Select precise recipes, resource pools, phase thresholds, and rules with the user
  when this work becomes active. No code or gameplay behavior changes here.
