Iron and diamond armor styles - 2026-10-12

Generated by tools/assets/expedition-armor.cjs using the existing model workflow.
Run node tools/assets/expedition-armor.cjs to regenerate. This regenerates all
expedition and cosmetic assets; transfer manual edits to the generator first.

Families: iron_classic, iron_viking, iron_spartan, diamond_classic,
diamond_viking, diamond_spartan.
Additional families: iron/diamond variants of war_chief, knight, berserker,
and samurai, using the same <metal>_<style> naming.

Wearable geometry: assets/darkspawn/geometry/armor/<family>.json
Material atlas: assets/darkspawn/textures/equipment/<family>.png
Inventory models: assets/darkspawn/models/item/<family>_<slot>.json and _trim.json
Editable review models: art/blockbench/<family>_armor/<family>.bbmodel
Vanilla item selectors: assets/minecraft/items/<metal>_<slot>.json
These paths under assets live in src/main/resources.

The native items select geometry through darkspawn:armor_style. No replacement
items, attribute changes, or equippable component overrides are used. Ordinary
armor with no component uses Classic. Unsupported items ignore the component.

Wearable trim-bearing cubes include trim_uv in native 64x32 texture coordinates.
The renderer uses a slightly inflated overlay and Minecraft's trim palettes and
patterns. Transparent base layers prevent repainting the armor. Inventory trims
use material-colored borders, not exact worn patterns. Custom material colors
fall back to the untrimmed item icon.

and Brawler artwork is archived, not craftable. final-roster-preview.png rows
are Paladin, Shadowblade, Ranger. Shadowblade uses a dark purple hood and
mask, edge spikes, purple wraps and heel blades; its entire armor render

legendary-preview.png: front/back pairs, Dragonlord and Phoenix on row one;
